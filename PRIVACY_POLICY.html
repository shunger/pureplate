1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

When you use Pure Pantry AI, you may provide:

  • Pantry & food data: Items, quantities, expiration dates, storage locations, categories
  • Recipes: Ingredients, instructions, photos, tags, ratings, and the name of the person a recipe came from
  • Shopping lists: Items, quantities, stores, purchase history, budget data
  • Meal plans: Planned meals, schedules, preferences
  • Family profile: Household size, children's age ranges, dietary preferences, restrictions and allergies
  • Messages to the AI chef: What you type or say, and any photos you attach
  • Product contributions: If you add a product the barcode database doesn't know, its name, brand and category
  • Account information: If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email address. Sign-in is optional, except that sharing a pantry or list with others requires it.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Anonymous account ID: The app creates an anonymous account the first time you open it, so AI features and your free trial work without signing in. It is a random identifier and does not include your name or email address.
  • Usage and subscription records: How many AI meal plans and chat messages you have used this week, when your free trial started, and whether you have an active Premium subscription.
  • Push notification token: If you allow notifications, a token that lets us send them to your device.
  • Device information: Device type, operating system version and app version, used to keep the service secure (Firebase App Check) and working.

We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Camera & Microphone

  • Camera: Barcode scanning happens on your device. When you send a recipe photo to the AI chef, for example a cookbook page you want saved as a recipe card, the photo is sent to our servers and to Amazon Web Services to read it (see Section 3). Photos you add to your own recipes are stored only on your device.
  • Microphone: Used for voice input in the AI chef and voice-guided cooking. Speech is turned into text by your device's speech recognition service (on iPhone, Apple's speech recognition, which may process audio on Apple's servers when on-device recognition is unavailable). Only the resulting text is sent to us.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide core app functionality: pantry tracking, recipe management, meal planning and shopping lists
  • Generate AI meal plans, recipes, recipe cards from photos, dish photos and spoken cooking guidance (see Section 3)
  • Look up product information from barcodes (Open Food Facts, UPC Item Database)
  • Sync the pantries and shopping lists you share with the people you share them with
  • Send push notifications you enable (expiry alerts, sharing activity, reminders)
  • Enforce free-tier limits and manage your subscription status
  • Protect the service from abuse

3. AI Features

Pure Pantry AI's AI features run on Amazon Web Services (AWS) through our Firebase Cloud Functions. Each request sends only what that feature needs:

FeatureWhat is sent
Meal plansYour preferences, family profile (household size, children's age ranges, dietary needs and allergies), pantry items, favorite recipes and recent ratings
AI chef chatYour message, the recent conversation, and your pantry items and preferences
Recipe photosThe photos you attach, so the recipe can be read and saved as a recipe card
Dish photosA recipe's name, ingredients and steps, used to draw a picture of the finished dish
Voice-guided cookingThe recipe's steps and your spoken questions (as text); the spoken replies are generated by Amazon Polly

Requests are not linked to your name or email address; they carry your anonymous account ID so we can apply usage limits. Results are returned to the app and saved on your device. We do not store your requests or photos on our servers, except that our servers keep short technical logs, which can include small excerpts of a request (such as the first lines of text read from a recipe photo), for up to 30 days to diagnose problems. AWS does not use this content to train its models.

AI-generated content, including recipes, nutrition estimates and dish photos, may be inaccurate. Always check ingredients against your family's allergies and dietary needs.

4. Data Storage

On Your Device

Your pantry, recipes, recipe photos, meal plans, shopping lists and family profile are stored on your device in a local database.

In the Cloud

  • Shared pantries and shopping lists: If you sign in and share a pantry or list, its contents, members and activity are stored in Firebase Firestore (operated by Google).
  • Account records: Your AI usage counts, free-trial start date, Premium status and push notification token are stored in Firebase under your account ID. To link a subscription to your account, we store a one-way (hashed) identifier of the purchase, not the receipt itself.
  • Product contributions: Products you add to the shared barcode database are stored in Firebase Firestore.

Data Retention

Data on your device stays until you delete it or remove the app. Cloud data is kept until you delete it or ask us to delete your account. Server logs are deleted after 30 days.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data to third parties.

We do not share your data for advertising purposes.

Data is shared only in these circumstances:

  • User-initiated sharing: When you share a pantry or shopping list, or join one with an invite code, its contents and activity feed are visible to all of its members. Members see your display name next to your membership and the changes you make. If you signed in with Google or Apple, this is the name on that account, but your email address is never shown. Members may be using Pure Pantry AI or Smart Shopping Scanner, our companion app, which can open the same shared pantries and lists.
  • Shared barcode database: Products you contribute (name, brand, category) can be seen by all users of Pure Pantry AI and Smart Shopping Scanner. They are stored with your anonymous account ID, not your name.
  • Recipes you share: When you share a recipe, it goes only to the people and apps you choose.
  • Service providers: The services listed in Section 5, strictly to run the app's features.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, regulation, or legal process.

7. Children's Privacy

Pure Pantry AI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The family profile can include children's age ranges so meal plans suit them; these ranges are sent with AI meal-plan requests (Section 3) but are not linked to any child's name.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, please contact us at support@purehungerlabs.com.

8. Your Rights

All Users

  • Delete data on your device: You can delete individual items, recipes, plans and lists in the app, or remove all of it by deleting the app.
  • Delete your account and cloud data: Email support@purehungerlabs.com with the subject "Pure Pantry AI account deletion". We complete deletions within 30 days. See Delete Your Account for exactly what is deleted and what is kept.
  • Cancel a subscription: Through the App Store or Google Play. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription.

California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information)
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@purehungerlabs.com.

9. Security

We protect your data through:

  • HTTPS encryption for all network communications
  • Firebase App Check to verify requests come from the genuine app
  • Device encryption provided by your operating system for data on your device
  • Access controls on cloud data: your account records can be read only by you, and the items in a shared pantry or list only by its members

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but take reasonable measures to protect your information.
