How to request deletion

Email support@purehungerlabs.com with the subject line "Pure Pantry AI account deletion".

  1. Send the email from the Google or Apple address you used to sign in to Pure Pantry AI. If you never signed in, say so and include the invite code of any shared pantry or list you joined so we can locate your data.
  2. State whether you want your entire account deleted, or only specific data (for example, just your shared pantry) while keeping your account.
  3. We will confirm your request by reply and complete the deletion within 30 days.

You can also remove all locally stored data yourself at any time by deleting the app from your device. Local data never leaves your device unless you use sharing features.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Subscription purchase records held by Apple or Google are managed by those stores under their own policies. Cancel an active subscription through the App Store or Google Play; deleting your account does not cancel it.
  • Product information you contributed to the shared barcode database (product name, brand, category) is kept because it is not linked to you after deletion. The reference to your account is removed.
  • Aggregate analytics that cannot identify you are retained.
  • Backups of cloud data are purged within 30 days of the deletion being completed.

For more about the data we collect, see the Pure Pantry AI Privacy Policy.
